// About

A blue-and-red-team background, built on labs I run end to end.

I'm a fourth-year Honours Bachelor of Information Technology student at Seneca Polytechnic in Toronto, specializing in cybersecurity and graduating in August 2027. I work across the full attack-and-defend lifecycle: emulating real adversaries, engineering detections, responding to intrusions, and hardening the environments I build.

In my co-op role at NRT Technology, a fintech operating under PCI DSS and SOC 2, I triage endpoint alerts with the security team, manage identity and endpoints across a hybrid Active Directory / Microsoft Entra ID environment, and automate operations with PowerShell. Outside of work, I build offensive and defensive labs that let me practice detection engineering, penetration testing, incident response, and threat hunting.

I bring an international perspective, with a background spanning Mauritius and Canada, and I'm bilingual in English and French. Years of volunteering and community engagement have shaped how I communicate, collaborate, and explain technical work clearly, which matters as much in security as the technical depth does.
