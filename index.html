// About

Identity is the new perimeter, and it's where I want to work.

I'm a fourth-year Honours Bachelor of Information Technology student at Seneca Polytechnic in Toronto, specializing in cybersecurity and graduating in August 2027. My focus is Identity and Access Management: the discipline of making sure the right people have the right access to the right resources, and nothing more.

In my co-op role at NRT Technology, a fintech operating under PCI DSS and SOC 2, I manage the full user identity lifecycle across a hybrid Active Directory / Microsoft Entra ID environment and automate onboarding and offboarding with PowerShell. Outside of work, I build IAM and detection labs that let me practice governance, least-privilege, and attack-and-defend scenarios end to end.

I bring an international perspective, with a background spanning Mauritius and Canada, and I'm bilingual in English and French. Years of volunteering and community engagement have shaped how I communicate, collaborate, and lead, skills that matter as much in security work as the technical depth does.
